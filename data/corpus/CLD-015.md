## Clauses
### Clause 2 - Hosting
Atlas Cloud Ltd shall host the Brightwater Foods plc ordering and warehouse systems in its UK data centres.

### Clause 3 - Uptime
Atlas Cloud Ltd shall keep the hosted systems available at least 99.9% of each month.

### Clause 5 - Fees
Brightwater Foods plc shall pay Atlas Cloud Ltd a monthly fee of 12,000 GBP within 30 days of each invoice.

### Clause 8 - Termination
Either party may end this agreement with 90 days written notice.
